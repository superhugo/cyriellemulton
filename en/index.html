Physiotherapy & yoga in Pantin

Every body, every gender, every story.

I am Cyrielle Multon, a physiotherapist specialising in pelvic floor rehabilitation, paediatrics and scar treatment, and a yoga teacher. My intention: to help you know your body better so that you can regain confidence in it.

I do not believe in ready-made protocols. I start from you, from your history, from what you feel, and I explain clearly what is known today. Here, there is nothing to achieve and no one to judge you: we move forward together, until you no longer need me.
